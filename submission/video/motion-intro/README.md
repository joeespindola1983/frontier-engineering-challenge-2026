# WAKE motion-design opening

These SVG layers support two preserved opening treatments. The original
7.5-second evidence-convergence concept appears in
`wake-final-submission-draft-v2.mp4`. The later brand-only treatment uses
`brand-hero.svg`, `tagline-hero.svg`, and `wave.svg` so the first narration can
carry the evidence story without repetition.

The visual sequence expresses the product model:

1. a training plan supplies intent;
2. SpeedCoach supplies measured performance;
3. athlete and coach input supplies human context;
4. WAKE reconciles the evidence into accountable club memory.

The files were created locally for this submission. No stock artwork, stock
footage, external logo asset, or third-party music is embedded in the opening.
The low ambient sound is synthesized from filtered noise and tones. This
sequence is an explanatory product animation, not evidence of a live agent
execution.
